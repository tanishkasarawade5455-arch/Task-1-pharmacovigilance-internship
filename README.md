# Task-1-pharmacovigilance-internship
ADR Identification Basics
ADR Identification Basics

Executive Summary
Eighteen patient case scenarios were assessed for potential adverse drug reactions (ADRs). Causality assessment was based on chronology, known pharmacology, dechallenge/rechallenge findings, concomitant medicines and clinical outcomes.

Objectives
Identify suspect medicines, correlate symptom onset with therapy initiation, distinguish suspect products from co-medications and prepare structured ADR documentation.

Methodology
Temporal association, expectedness, seriousness, dechallenge and rechallenge information were reviewed. Positive rechallenge was considered strong evidence for causality.

Data Quality Review
Several records contain unusual doses and nonspecific indications. Exact calendar dates and detailed medical history were unavailable.

Aggregate Findings
Most reactions matched the known safety profile of the suspect medicine. Amoxicillin-rash, Amlodipine-peripheral oedema, Ciprofloxacin-tendon pain and Atorvastatin-myalgia demonstrated the strongest signals.
